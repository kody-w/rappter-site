Content repurposing system for a synthetic founder interview

A static content-system proof that transforms a wholly synthetic interview into an original article outline, short-post set, email brief, and reusable editorial rules.

The job

Customer problem

A founder records useful long-form conversations but has no repeatable method for turning one approved source into channel-specific material without losing accuracy or voice.

Synthetic inputs

Synthetic interview

A fictional founder explains how a neighborhood repair service reduced missed appointments by confirming time windows and assigning one owner to every exception.

Voice constraints

Direct, practical, no hype, no guaranteed results, and no invented customer quotations.

Channel brief

One article outline, three short posts, one email summary, and one reusable call-to-action pattern.

What the AI did

  1. Extracted only claims present in the synthetic source.
  2. Separated reusable ideas from source-specific details.
  3. Assigned one communication job to each target channel.
  4. Generated original representative drafts under the voice constraints.
  5. Checked every draft for unsupported claims, copied phrasing, and prohibited guarantees.

Findings and deliverables

  • The strongest reusable idea is assigning explicit ownership to workflow exceptions.
  • Each channel needs a distinct opening and depth rather than a truncated copy of the same text.
  • A source ledger prevents derivative drafts from silently inventing proof.
  • The call to action should invite a bounded workflow review rather than promise a business result.

Acceptance checks

PASS

Every factual claim traces to the synthetic source

The representative drafts use only the fictional interview facts and declared editorial rules.

PASS

Outputs are meaningfully channel-specific

The article, short posts, and email use different structures, lengths, and reader actions.

PASS

No third-party protected source is used

The complete source and all names in the proof are synthetic.

What remains unproven

  • The proof does not establish performance, reach, engagement, or brand fit with a real audience.
  • A production package would require a source the customer owns or is authorized to use.

Next access request
