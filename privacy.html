Collect less. Prove safely.

RapterBox LLC operates the AI Contractor Beta. This notice covers the public application and privacy-request forms on rappter.com.

What we collect

  • Name and email address.

Do not submit credentials, regulated data, customer records, confidential documents, attachments, or production-system details.

Why we use it

We use application information to evaluate fit, communicate about a request, construct a synthetic equivalent, prepare a proof, operate security and abuse controls, and keep necessary business records. We do not sell beta application information or treat it as permission to publish a case study.

Service providers and retention

GitHub Pages serves the public site, Formspree delivers form submissions, and Shopify operates the password-protected store. Their own terms and privacy notices also apply to their processing.

First-cohort records are reviewed manually and retained only while needed to evaluate or operate the request, respond to a privacy or security issue, keep required business records, or meet legal obligations. No fixed deletion period is promised for records that must be retained.

Your choices

You may ask for access, correction, or deletion of application information. Some information may be retained when required for security, dispute, accounting, or legal reasons. A request does not require beta participation.

Do not include identity documents, account passwords, customer data, or other sensitive attachments.
