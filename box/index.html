RBOX MOBILE · MK0 · RUNS IN THIS BROWSER

rbox mobile. This tab is your box: it thinks with a key you bring and keeps every receipt on this device.

Same routines as the office box, same receipts, same checks. Nothing leaves this browser except two public reads from GitHub (the method page and the sample data, pinned to a commit) and the request to the model you choose — your key is sent only there and never to us. When you want the box somewhere else, pack its chain into an .egg and hatch it on a phone, a laptop, or a real box in your office. Everything here is the sample business; nothing of yours is read.
