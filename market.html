THE FIRST HATCH PROMISE

Seven days. No reason required.

Return the purchased organism during the protected window and RapterBox takes current title and the transferable Capsule back into inventory, then refunds the purchase price through the original payment path.

Your private life is not part of the return. Credentials, private memory, customer records, private prompts, and independently minted offspring do not come back with the parent.

External taxes, processor fees, currency conversion, and network charges are refunded to the extent recoverable or legally required. Fraud, duplicate refunds, sanctions, chargeback abuse, and prohibited transactions remain refusable.

ONE FIXED SET / MANY TRANSFERS

Originals do not multiply

The 251 First Edition Originals move between holders and RapterBox inventory. Resale does not mint another Original.

Offspring stay distinct

Descendants carry their own RAPPIDs, histories, rights, and supply. They are never represented as duplicate Originals.

Private state stays private

The market moves title, declared transferable artifacts, public lineage, and stated rights—not credentials or private memory.

RAPTERBOX AS MARKET MAKER

A floor is a real bid, not a story.

RapterBox may publish standing bids or direct buyout offers. Every company bid names the covered organisms, price, quantity or budget, expiry, condition, and settlement terms. While funded and active, that bid is an observable floor only for the inventory it actually covers.

Transparent conflict

RapterBox is issuer, official market operator, and possible bidder. Every company bid says so.

Atomic settlement

Accepted offers transfer exact current title and declared artifacts to RapterBox inventory.

No investment promise

No equity, dividend, guaranteed appreciation, guaranteed liquidity, or permanent buyout floor.

PRELAUNCH BOUNDARY

The policy is public before the market is open.

All Originals remain issuer-held, untransferred, and undiscovered. Commercial launch still requires rights clearance, an auditable selection mechanism, payments review, and age or location controls where required.
