Questions people ask before they buy

Does it send emails or sign things for me?

No. It never sends, signs, pays or deletes on your behalf — the software has no way to do those things. A task needing one comes back to you as a decision, with the reversible work already finished.

What happens if it cannot see everything?

It refuses to act on partial evidence. It stops and says so rather than guessing confidently — a confident wrong answer is worse than no answer.

What happens when the internet drops?

The box keeps working and catches up when the connection comes back. If you need every office to see the same thing at the same second, this is the wrong box.

Who owns the records?

You do. The box runs on your network. The records can only be added to, never changed or erased, and your accountant or insurer can check them without trusting us.

Does it need cloud AI?

It runs on the box and uses one GitHub Copilot seat, which you buy directly for about $10–20 a month. There is no separate cloud sandbox to meter and we never resell AI usage to you.

Why the best box, and why is it on the receipt?

Best for the best. The first ten businesses get the best Mac mini Apple makes, because a slow twin is a bad product and we will not ship one. You pay Apple's price and we hand you the receipt — no markup, we make our money on the service. It is yours to keep, and inside 30 days we take it back with the refund.

Why only ten?

Because every install day is done in person by the founder, and the first ten businesses get a 20-minute call every month. Ten is what one person can place well. The counter at the top is the live count.
